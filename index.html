FAQ

Common questions.

Everything you need to know before booking your first clean.

How does the booking work?
Click "Book Now" to open our Square booking calendar. You'll see real-time available slots — pick your date and time and you're confirmed instantly. No phone calls, no waiting, no back-and-forth. Square sends you an email and text confirmation immediately.
Do I need to be home during the service?
Not at all. You just need to ensure your outdoor water connection (spigot) is accessible before we arrive. We handle everything else. Many customers aren't home during their service.
Do you bring your own water?
We connect directly to your outdoor water connection — no water truck or tank needed. Just make sure your outdoor spigot is accessible and operational on the day of service. We bring all other equipment.
What hours do you operate?
We operate Monday–Friday 7:00am–1:00pm, with extended hours Thursday & Friday until 4:00pm, plus Saturday & Sunday 9:00am–2:00pm. You can see exact available slots when you open the booking calendar.
Are your cleaning products safe for kids and pets?
Absolutely. We exclusively use 100% biodegradable, non-toxic cleaning solutions that are safe for children, pets, and Miami's environment. All gray water is handled responsibly in compliance with Miami-Dade guidelines.
Do I have to sign up for a subscription?
Never. You can book one-time cleanings or a monthly plan — your call. Either way there are no cancellation fees, ever, and no hidden fees. Cancel anytime. We earn your repeat business through the quality of our work.
