Who We Are

trusted bin & exterior crew

Prestige Bin Services keeps Miami homes looking — and smelling — their best. We connect to your outdoor spigot and give every bin a full interior and exterior pressure wash, deep sanitization, and odor elimination using commercial-grade, eco-friendly, pet-safe products.
